**Olist E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis**

**Business Case: Reallocating Marketing Budgets**
The objective of this analysis is to identify which Brazilian cities generate the highest revenue for Olist to optimize marketing ad spend distribution.

**The SQL Methodology**
Using DuckDB inside Python, I joined the relational `orders`, `customers`, and `items` datasets to pull the top 10 core metropolitan areas.

**Key Findings & Revenue Visualization**
The analysis shows that **São Paulo** generates the highest total volume, but regional hubs like **Salvador** and **Rio de Janeiro** boast higher Average Order Values (AOV).

**Strategic Business Recommendation**
Do not distribute ad budget evenly across Brazil. We recommend maintaining dominant scale in São Paulo, but spinning up premium marketing campaigns in **Salvador** and **Rio de Janeiro** to exploit their higher spending trends.
